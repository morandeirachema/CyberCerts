# Practice Platforms (legit, hands-on)

Use these alongside your self-hosted [`../labs/`](../labs/README.md) to build reps. All are legal, authorized environments — never practice on anything outside them or your own lab.

> 🧪 **See also:** [labs/practice-ranges.md](../labs/practice-ranges.md) for a longer, sourced discussion of each range.

## Guided / beginner-friendly
- TryHackMe — https://tryhackme.com/  (guided rooms; has CEH-adjacent and beginner paths)
- OverTheWire wargames (Bandit → Natas) — https://overthewire.org/wargames/  (free, pure CLI/web fundamentals)
- PortSwigger Web Security Academy — https://portswigger.net/web-security  (free, best-in-class for Modules 14–15)
- picoCTF — https://picoctf.org/  (free CTF practice)

## Intermediate / realistic boxes
- Hack The Box — https://www.hackthebox.com/  (labs + Academy modules)
- VulnHub — https://www.vulnhub.com/  (downloadable vulnerable VMs for your hypervisor)
- Metasploitable 2 — https://docs.rapid7.com/metasploit/metasploitable-2/  (already in your Vagrant lab)
- OWASP Juice Shop — https://owasp.org/www-project-juice-shop/  (already in your Docker lab)
- DVWA — https://github.com/digininja/DVWA  (already in your Docker lab)

## Blue-team / detection cross-training (leverages your PAM background)
- LetsDefend — https://letsdefend.io/
- CyberDefenders — https://cyberdefenders.org/
- MITRE ATT&CK Navigator — https://mitre-attack.github.io/attack-navigator/

## Exam-style question practice
Prefer sources that *teach* (explanations per answer) over dumps. Confirm they map to CEH v13. Track your scores in [`../PROGRESS.md`](../PROGRESS.md).

> If a platform ever asks you to attack a target outside its own scope, stop — that's the line between practice and a crime.
