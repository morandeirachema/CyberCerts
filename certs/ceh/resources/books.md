# Books & Study Frameworks

Editions and availability change — verify the current CEH v13 edition before buying, and prefer the latest printing.

## Aligned to CEH
- **CEH v13 official courseware / iLabs** (EC-Council) — the authoritative source; comes with official training. https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/certified-ethical-hacker-ceh/
- **CEH Certified Ethical Hacker All-in-One Exam Guide** (Matt Walker, McGraw-Hill) — popular third-party guide; confirm the edition matches v13.
- **CEH Certified Ethical Hacker Study Guide / Practice Tests** (Sybex) — confirm current edition.

## Foundational (fill gaps the exam assumes)
- **The Web Application Hacker's Handbook** (Stuttard & Pinto) — pairs with Modules 14–15 and PortSwigger Academy.
- **Hacking: The Art of Exploitation** (Jon Erickson) — low-level fundamentals.
- **The Hacker Playbook 3** (Peter Kim) — practical methodology.
- **RTFM: Red Team Field Manual** — command quick-reference.

## Standards you can read free (better than any book for definitions)
- NIST SP 800-115 — https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final
- OWASP Testing Guide — https://owasp.org/www-project-web-security-testing-guide/
- FIRST CVSS spec — https://www.first.org/cvss/

## Note-taking framework for this repo
- Keep raw command output and screenshots in a **git-ignored** `loot/` or `captures/` folder (already in `.gitignore`).
- Summaries and lessons go into each module's **📝 My lab log** table.
- Weak areas → [`../PROGRESS.md`](../PROGRESS.md) weak-area log; re-drill until ≥80%.
- Consider a spaced-repetition deck (Anki) for ports, hashcat modes, and tool→purpose pairs.
