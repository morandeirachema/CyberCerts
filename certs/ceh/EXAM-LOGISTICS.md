## 1. Eligibility (two paths)

EC-Council offers two routes to sit the exam:

1. **Official training** — Attend official CEH training (iClass, an Accredited Training Center, or approved academic partner). Training usually includes an exam voucher and removes the application step.
2. **Self-study / challenge the exam** — Without official training you must submit an **Eligibility Application** showing **at least 2 years of work experience in information security** (or equivalent), plus a non-refundable application fee, and get approved before buying a voucher.

## 5. Scheduling & ID

- Book Knowledge exam through Pearson VUE — https://home.pearsonvue.com/eccouncil
- Government-issued photo ID required; name must match your registration exactly.
- For online proctoring, check webcam/room/environment requirements in advance.

## 6. Results & retakes

- Knowledge exam results are shown at the test center / on completion; a score report follows in the EC-Council portal.
- **Retake policy:** EC-Council enforces waiting periods and attempt limits per 12-month window. **Verify:** current retake policy = `__________`

## 7. Keeping the cert (ECE / recertification)

- CEH is **ANSI/ISO 17024 accredited** and valid for **3 years**.
- Maintain it with **ECE (EC-Council Continuing Education) credits** — **120 credits over 3 years** — plus an annual membership fee.
- ECE policy — https://cert.eccouncil.org/ece-policy.html
