## 1. Eligibility (two paths)

EC-Council offers two routes to sit the exam:

1. **Official training** — Attend official CEH training (iClass, an Accredited Training Center, or approved academic partner). Training usually includes an exam voucher and removes the application step.
2. **Self-study / challenge the exam** — Without official training you must submit an **Eligibility Application** showing **at least 2 years of work experience in information security** (or equivalent), plus a non-refundable application fee, and get approved before buying a voucher.

- Official eligibility page — https://cert.eccouncil.org/ (Application process)
- **Verify:** current application fee = **USD 100, non-refundable** (cert.eccouncil.org application-process page, read 2026-09-09). The approved application is valid for 3 months; the voucher bought within that window is valid for one year.
- **Verify:** experience-in-lieu-of-training exact wording = *"two years of work experience in an Information Security role"* (cert.eccouncil.org application-process page, read 2026-09-09).

## 2. The two exams

| | CEH (Knowledge) | CEH (Practical) |
|---|---|---|
| Code | 312-50 (v13 = 312-50v13) | CEH Practical |
| Type | 125 multiple choice | 20 hands-on challenges |
| Time | 4 hours | 6 hours |
| Pass | Variable cut score (~60–85%) | 60–85% per form (verified 2026-09-09) |
| Where | Pearson VUE / ECC Exam Center | EC-Council Cyber Range (online, proctored) |
| Earns | CEH | CEH Master (with Knowledge exam) |

- **Verify:** current Practical passing threshold = **60% to 85%** (cut-score varies by form; CEH Practical page, read 2026-09-09). 20 challenges, 6 hours, proctored by EC-Council, delivered in the iLabs Cyber Range via the Aspen dashboard.

## 5. Scheduling & ID

- Book Knowledge exam through Pearson VUE — https://home.pearsonvue.com/eccouncil
- Government-issued photo ID required; name must match your registration exactly.
- For online proctoring, check webcam/room/environment requirements in advance.

## 6. Results & retakes

- Knowledge exam results are shown at the test center / on completion; a score report follows in the EC-Council portal.
- **Retake policy** (cert.eccouncil.org exam-retake-policy page, read 2026-09-09): no waiting period before the first retake; **14 days** before the third, fourth and fifth attempts; **12 months** before a sixth; **no more than five attempts per 12-month period**; a candidate who passes may not re-sit the same exam version. EC-Council advises official hands-on training after a third failure.
  - Retake policy page — https://cert.eccouncil.org/exam-retake-policy.html

## 7. Keeping the cert (ECE / recertification)

- CEH is **ANSI/ISO 17024 accredited** and valid for **3 years**.
- Maintain it with **ECE (EC-Council Continuing Education) credits** — **120 credits over 3 years** — plus an annual membership fee.
- ECE policy — https://cert.eccouncil.org/ece-policy.html
- **Verify:** current annual membership fee = **USD 80** for holders of at least one certification under the continuing-education policy (USD 20 otherwise); 120 credits per certification per three-year cycle (cert.eccouncil.org policy page, read 2026-09-09).
