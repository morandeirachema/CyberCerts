# CySA+ (CS0-003) — Coverage Map

This page is **Step 2** of the repo's [how to prepare a certification](../../../learning/how-to-prepare-a-cert.md)
method: the **contract between you and the exam**. The official objectives document lists
everything CompTIA can ask; this map lists everything this hub teaches. Lining the two up
tells you, before you study, what is covered, what is thin, and what is missing.

The rows are the **section headings that exist in this hub's four domain pages** — nothing
more. The official objective numbers are **not** reproduced here (the hub does not carry
CompTIA's numbering, and this page will not invent it), so the first column is yours to fill.

**How to fill it in:**

1. Download the official **CS0-003 exam objectives PDF** (or the **CS0-004** PDF if you will sit
   after the CS0-003 English retirement on 2026-12-22 — see the
   [version notice](../00-overview/exam-and-objectives.md#version-notice--cs0-003-retires-cs0-004-is-live)) — the steps are in
   [exam & objectives → how to get the official exam objectives](../00-overview/exam-and-objectives.md#how-to-get-the-official-exam-objectives).
2. Walk the PDF objective by objective. For each one, find the matching row below and write the
   official number (as printed in the PDF) in the first column. One objective may span several
   rows, and one row may cover several objectives — record all of them.
3. Any objective with **no matching row** goes in the [gaps table](#gaps-you-found) at the end,
   marked **"gap"**, with a note on where you will learn it.
4. Then score every row **0–3** (the scale is in
   [Step 3 of the method](../../../learning/how-to-prepare-a-cert.md#step-3--self-assess)) and tick
   *Practised?* only after you have done the hands-on item for that topic.

> The numbered sections in the domain pages (*1. … 6.*) are the **hub's** numbering, not
> CompTIA's. Domain weights in the group headings are the ones the hub records from CompTIA's
> page *(verify on CompTIA — weightings change per exam version)*.

## How to use

- **Before studying:** fill the first column and the self-score column; the zeros and ones are
  your syllabus. Domains 1 and 2 together carry 63% of the exam — start there.
- **During the study loop:** after each domain page, revisit its rows — raise the score, tick
  *Practised?* (for CySA+ that means reading real logs, scanner output and packet captures,
  not just the page), and use *Notes* for the weak-area log entry.
- **Before booking:** the readiness gate (Step 6 of the method) needs every row at 2 or 3 and
  the gaps table empty or answered.
- **Where a row has a `↳`**, it is a subsection of the row above it; score it separately only if
  the PDF treats it as its own objective.
- Rows link straight to the section, so this page doubles as the hub's fastest index.
