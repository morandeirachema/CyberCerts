    Security+ (SY0-701) — Coverage Map¶

    This page is Step 2 of the repo's how to prepare a certification method: the contract between you and the exam. The official objectives document lists everything CompTIA can ask; this map lists everything this hub teaches. Lining the two up tells you, before you study, what is covered, what is thin, and what is missing.

    The rows are the section headings that exist in this hub's five domain pages — nothing more. The official objective numbers are not reproduced here (the hub does not carry CompTIA's numbering, and this page will not invent it), so the first column is yours to fill.

    How to fill it in:

    1. Download the official SY0-701 exam objectives PDF — the steps are in exam & objectives → how to get the official exam objectives.
    2. Walk the PDF objective by objective. For each one, find the matching row below and write the official number (as printed in the PDF) in the first column. One objective may span several rows, and one row may cover several objectives — record all of them.
    3. Any objective with no matching row goes in the gaps table at the end, marked "gap", with a note on where you will learn it.
    4. Then score every row 0–3 (the scale is in Step 3 of the method) and tick Practised? only after you have done the hands-on item for that topic.

    The Domain 3 and Domain 4 pages number their sections 3.1 … 3.4 and 4.1 … 4.12 in the style of CompTIA's objective areas. Treat those as the hub's section numbers: confirm each against the PDF before copying it into the first column. Domain weights in the group headings are the ones the hub records from CompTIA's page (verify on CompTIA — weightings change per exam version).

    How to use¶

    • Before studying: fill the first column and the self-score column; the zeros and ones are your syllabus. Study the weight-heavy domains first (4 → 2 → 5 → 3 → 1), unless a lower-weight domain is a prerequisite — Domain 1 is the vocabulary, so the hub's plan starts there.
    • During the study loop: after each domain page, revisit its rows — raise the score, tick Practised?, and use Notes for the weak-area log entry (what you got wrong, fixed?).
    • Before booking: the readiness gate (Step 6 of the method) needs every row at 2 or 3 and the gaps table empty or answered.
    • Where a row has a ↳, it is a subsection of the row above it; score it separately only if the PDF treats it as its own objective.
    • Rows link straight to the section, so this page doubles as the hub's fastest index.
